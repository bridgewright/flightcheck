# Forward Deployed Product Manager

**Meridian Flow — Enterprise AI Workflows**
Location: Singapore or remote APAC · Full-time

## About Meridian Flow

Meridian Flow builds AI agents that run the back-office workflows of
mid-market logistics and finance teams. We are a values-driven company: we
believe in responsible innovation, ethical AI that benefits everyone, and
technology built with integrity. Our culture is grounded in trust,
transparency, and doing the right thing even when nobody is watching. We
take our mission seriously: AI should amplify human judgment, never
replace it.

## What you will do

- Deploy Meridian Flow's agent platform inside enterprise customer
  environments, owning the rollout from first workshop to steady state.
- Run discovery with operations leads and translate what you find into
  agent configurations the customer can run without you.
- Turn field learnings into product direction: write the specs that come
  back from deployments and argue for them against the core roadmap.
- Own the deployment metrics for your accounts: time to first automated
  workflow, weekly active workflows, and expansion revenue.
- Work beside solutions engineers on-site; you are the product voice in
  the room and the customer's voice back at headquarters.

## What we look for

- 4+ years of product management experience, with at least one year on a
  product that ships LLM features to production.
- Evidence you have owned a customer-facing deployment end to end:
  discovery, configuration, launch, adoption.
- Comfort presenting to VP-level stakeholders and running a room of
  skeptical operators.
- You handle ambiguity by shipping something small and measuring it.
- Willingness to travel up to 40% across APAC.

## What we offer

Competitive salary and equity, an annual learning budget, and a team that
sends you home on time.
