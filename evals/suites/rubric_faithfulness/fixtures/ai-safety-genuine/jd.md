# Product Manager, Safety Evaluations

**Windrose Research**
Location: London · Hybrid · Full-time

## The role

Windrose Research builds evaluation infrastructure for frontier AI labs.
Our safety evaluation suite is the product: it is how our customers decide
whether a model is safe to ship. You will own it.

## What you will do

- Own the roadmap for the safety evaluation suite: red-teaming harnesses,
  refusal benchmarks, and misuse-risk scoring.
- Design red-teaming programs with our customers' safety teams and turn
  what they find into product requirements.
- Translate responsible-AI policy requirements, including regulatory
  frameworks, into concrete evaluation criteria engineers can build.
- Publish evaluation methodology openly and defend it to external
  researchers.
- Prioritize the eval backlog against customer model-release deadlines.

## What we look for

- Product management experience on developer tools, evaluation systems,
  or ML infrastructure.
- Working knowledge of AI safety literature: red-teaming, alignment
  evaluations, misuse taxonomies.
- Evidence you can hold a quality bar under release pressure and say no
  with a straight face.
- Clear technical writing; your PRDs are read by researchers.

## Working here

We are a research-driven company of forty people. Expect direct feedback,
public write-ups, and a strong opinion about measurement.
