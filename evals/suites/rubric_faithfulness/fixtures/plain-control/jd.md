# Senior Product Analyst

**Harbor Lane Logistics**
Location: Rotterdam · Full-time

## About us

Harbor Lane moves refrigerated freight across northern Europe. We care
about our people, our community, and running a sustainable business that
our drivers are proud of.

## What you will do

- Own growth analytics for the booking platform: funnels, cohorts, and
  the weekly numbers review the leadership team runs on.
- Design and analyze experiments on pricing and carrier matching.
- Build and maintain the dashboards operations managers use daily.
- Partner with product managers to turn analysis into roadmap decisions.
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders and defend the method.

## What we look for

- 5+ years in product or data analytics with strong SQL.
- Experiment design experience: you can explain power, interference, and
  when an A/B test is the wrong tool.
- A portfolio of analyses that changed a product decision.
- Clear communication with operators who do not read dashboards.

## Benefits

Solid salary, profit share, 30 days of leave, and a bike plan.
