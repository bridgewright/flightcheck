## What the new suite's first live run showed

The pathological fixture is the point of the release. Its findings are
deliberately salted with responsible-AI interview articles and its JD's only
ethics content is About-section boilerplate — the exact configuration that
produced the production rubric whose top-weighted dimension was mission
alignment. Compiled under the new contract, it produced five content
dimensions (deployment ownership, field discovery, stakeholder management,
metrics ownership, ambiguity) — **zero ethics, values, or mission dimensions
in either channel** — with every content dimension carrying evidence
verbatim from the JD's responsibilities and requirements.

The mirror fixture proves the rule is licensing, not a topic ban: the
AI-safety JD compiled to red-teaming design and AI-safety domain knowledge
at honest weights, because there the role itself demands them. The plain
control compiled to pure analytics dimensions, with nothing drawn from its
community-and-sustainability About section.

The run also proves what no fake-client test could: the response schema
with nullable `jd_evidence` is accepted live by the model API, and the live
compiler satisfied the deterministic checks (exact-substring evidence
against the truncated-and-neutralized JD, delivery band 1-3) on the first
attempt for all three fixtures.

## Honest limits

Three fixtures cannot distinguish a prompt that reliably refuses unlicensed
dimensions from one that got lucky three times; the suite's value is that
the burned configuration is now a committed regression test that runs at
every tag. The discrimination suites remain at the small sizes stated since
v0.7; F-05 (golden set 3 to 9 triplets) is still the card that makes those
numbers statistically honest.
