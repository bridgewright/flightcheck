## What this gate does and does not cover

Between v0.2.0 and this tag the judged path changed, so the run matters rather
than merely repeating. What it exercises, precisely:

- **Covered.** Layer 1 (`scorer.evals_l1.golden.judge_discrimination`) runs the
  content judge over the committed golden triplets, so the v0.5 delimiter
  fencing of user-controlled text at the judge prompt site is exercised: the
  judge still separates strong from borderline from weak at 1.0 with the fences
  in place. Layer 3 runs the delivery judge and the DSP path over the committed
  clip triplets.
- **Not covered, and stated rather than implied.** Layer 1 scores against the
  committed `evals/suites/rubric_discrimination/rubric.json` and does not
  invoke the rubric compiler. The v0.5 JD truncation that runs *before* the
  compiler, and the hidden-HTML strip at intake, therefore have unit tests but
  no eval-gate coverage. An injection eval suite that would close this gap is
  scheduled as part of F-11b in v0.6.
- **Unchanged limitation.** N=3 triplets at layer 1 and 2 at layer 3. One
  boundary case still decides the gate, exactly as the v0.2 report recorded.
  Growing the golden set stays queued; nothing here should be read as a
  precision estimate.
