## What changed since the last gate

**The gated surface grew for the first time since v0.1.** `injection_defence`
is new to the gate (v0.6, F-11b) and this is its **first release run** — it was
added to `evals_l3/regression.py` twenty-two minutes after `evals/README.md` was
last written, which is why that README described a two-suite gate until this
batch corrected it.

It gates in both directions, and the strict one is the ceiling rather than the
floor: `hostile_recall ≥ 0.9` catches instruction sets, and
`benign_false_positive_rate ≤ 0.0` catches the failure that actually happened —
the first classifier refused real prompt-engineering job postings, which is the
product's core input. A single benign false positive fails this gate. There were
none across 16 benign fixtures.

## What this gate does and does not cover

- **Covered.** Layer 1 runs the content judge over the committed golden triplets
  with the delimiter fencing in place. Layer 3 runs the delivery judge and the
  DSP path over the committed clip triplets. `injection_defence` runs the intake
  classifier over 13 hostile and 16 benign fixtures, all committed — so unlike
  the other two suites, **a fresh clone reproduces this one in full and needs no
  API key.**
- **Not covered, and stated rather than implied.** Layer 1 scores against the
  committed `evals/suites/rubric_discrimination/rubric.json` and does not invoke
  the rubric compiler. Nothing in this gate exercises the v0.6 pipeline
  resilience work (backoff, the concurrency limit, the job deadline, the dead
  letter) — those have unit tests and no eval coverage, because they are
  failure-path behaviour rather than judgement quality. Nothing here exercises
  the F-21 design pass at all; that batch's gates are in `apps/web`'s test
  suite.
- **Unchanged limitation, and it is the important one.** N=3 triplets at layer 1
  and 2 at layer 3. **One boundary case still decides two of the three suites.**
  A 1.0 on three trials is not a precision estimate and nothing here should be
  read as one. Growing the golden set stays queued as F-05. The injection suite
  is the one with a sample worth the name, at 29 fixtures.
