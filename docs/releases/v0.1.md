# flightcheck v0.1.0

Released: 2026-07-26

## What works

- **JD intake → cited rubric.** Paste a JD (or its URL), optionally add a resume
  PDF and a LinkedIn Save-to-PDF export. The worker researches how the role
  actually interviews (grounded search, real citations) and compiles a BARS
  rubric of 5–8 weighted dimensions — every dimension carries at least one
  source citation, captured at compile time (see the citation-link caveat in
  Known limits).
- **A real voice interview.** A 20-minute browser session over WebRTC with an
  OpenAI Realtime interviewer that asks one question at a time, forces
  specifics, and challenges one answer. Hard cut at 25 minutes.
- **Dual-channel scoring from the actual audio.** Content is judged from the
  verbatim transcript against the rubric's anchors, with evidence quotes
  verified to appear in the transcript. Delivery is judged from the recording
  itself and cross-checked against measured DSP metrics (pace, fillers,
  silences >= 1s, response latency); disagreements are flagged as DSP
  conflicts, not hidden.
- **An honest report.** Verdict (not ready yet / approaching / ready), overall
  and per-dimension scores, a timestamped observations timeline, strengths,
  gaps against the score-5 anchors, and two concrete drills.

## Measured evals (release gate: `scorer-evals` exit 0)

| Suite | Metric | Result | Baseline | Run date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Layer 1 — rubric discrimination | blind-rank accuracy | 1.0 | 0.8 | 2026-07-26 |
| Layer 3 — delivery discrimination | judge accuracy | 1.0 | 0.8 | 2026-07-26 |

The gate's JSON outputs are committed verbatim in
[`evals/reports/2026-07-26-v01-gate.md`](../../evals/reports/2026-07-26-v01-gate.md)
(the generated `evals/out/` directory itself is gitignored). DSP separability
is recorded there but not gated in v0.1 (no committed baseline yet).

The layer-1 number was not free: the first measured run scored 0.0. The judge
saturated strong and borderline answers at 5.0 in batched scoring, and single
calls were not reproducible even at temperature 0 with a fixed seed. The fix —
focused per-dimension calls, anchor-first scoring rules, and a three-sample
mean — is in the changelog; 1.0 is the stable result of that reworked judge on
three consecutive runs, with the human blind-rank agreeing with all three golden
triplets before any judge run was trusted. Sample-size caveat, stated
plainly: **N=3 triplets** — this is a mechanism-level signal that the judge
redesign fixed the failure mode, not a large-sample accuracy claim; growing
N is v0.2 work.

## Known limits

- **One baseline session per package.** The multi-session coaching arc
  (focused drills, re-tests) is not in v0.1.
- **No per-session cost metering yet.** Per-session cost metering and public
  unit economics (`docs/metrics/`) land in v0.2.
- **Interviewer is OpenAI Realtime only.** The Gemini Live browser path was
  evaluated during the W1 bake-off and kept as a lab harness (`webroom`), not
  the product session room; the open investigation into its session behavior
  is tracked in DECISIONS.md (#002) and will be revisited before any
  provider change.
- **Verdicts are calibration claims, not admission predictions.** This verdict
  reflects alignment with rubric anchors and an experienced-interviewer scoring
  protocol; it is not a prediction of any specific company's decision. The
  report never asserts inner states — only observed signals with timestamps.
- **English-only sessions.** The interviewer and the scoring protocol target
  English interviews; other languages are untested.
- **Model behavior can drift.** Scoring and interviewer models are pinned in
  config (`gpt-realtime`, `gemini-2.5-flash`) and provider behavior can drift;
  the evals gate exists to catch that drift before it reaches users.
- **Citation links can expire.** Grounded-search citations are captured at
  compile time as Google grounding redirect URLs, which are time-limited; the
  citation title carries the publisher domain so the source stays
  identifiable after a link expires. Resolving redirects to durable publisher
  URLs is planned for v0.2.
- **Session audio transits Google's Files API.** Scoring uploads the
  recording to the Gemini Files API (transcription and the delivery judge)
  and does not explicitly delete it; retention rides Google's automatic
  file expiry (~48 hours) on top of the private Supabase `recordings`
  bucket, which remains the recording's home.
