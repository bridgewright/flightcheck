# flightcheck v0.11.0

Released: 2026-08-05

The rubric proves itself now.

## What shipped

**Every content dimension must show its JD receipt** (F-62, DECISIONS 044).
The compiler used to ground dimensions in research findings and corpus
documents, and nothing required the job description itself to license one.
The failure that motivated this release: a production package compiled from
a 108-character scrape-failed JD came out as a seven-dimension rubric whose
top-weighted content dimension was mission-and-ethics alignment, licensed
entirely by research citations, and the session planner then guaranteed
that dimension a question slot in a paying customer's twenty minutes. Now
each content dimension carries `jd_evidence`: a verbatim quote from the
job description, at most 300 characters, enforced in code as an exact
substring of the JD exactly as the compiler saw it (truncated, then
marker-neutralized, through the same public helper on both the enforcement
and eval sides so the two cannot drift). Values boilerplate and research
articles corroborate; only the JD licenses. A genuine AI-safety JD still
earns its ethics dimension at full weight, because this is a licensing
rule, not a topic ban. Faithfulness failures share the compiler's single
repair retry, and delivery-channel dimensions are capped at three so the
channel label cannot become the dodge.

**The bar shows its receipts on /rubric.** Under each content dimension,
the JD sentence that earned it, quoted back in the posting's own words;
delivery dimensions carry the product's line instead ("This bar comes from
the product, not your JD: how you sound is part of every interview").
Rubrics compiled before the field existed render exactly as they always
have, pinned by tests that render the old shape byte-identical.

**A rubric-faithfulness suite joined the release gate.** Three committed JD
fixtures, all fictional companies: one salted with values-section
boilerplate and responsible-AI research articles, reproducing the
production failure; one genuine AI-safety role where an ethics dimension is
REQUIRED to appear; one plain control. Each compiles live at the gate with
a stubbed research sweep and is checked deterministically: every piece of
evidence verbatim in the processed JD, zero forbidden-topic dimensions in
either channel, required topics present, delivery count in band. The floor
is every fixture passing.

## What the merge caught

Per-track green is not combined green, again: the eval track's tests
authored unfaithful canned rubrics and fed them through the real compiler,
which was correct on its own branch and impossible on the merged tree,
where the compiler track's enforcement blocks exactly those shapes and
burns the fake's replies on repair retries. The fix states the tests'
actual purpose: the runner's checks exist to catch a regressed compiler,
so those tests now simulate one directly, while the faithful path still
exercises the real compiler end to end.

## What the reviews caught

Nine agents built the three tracks with two adversarial review rounds
each. Worth publishing: round 1 on the eval track proved the machinery
haystack was unpinned (comparing evidence against the raw JD instead of
the truncated-and-neutralized one left every test green) and pinned it
with a synthetic marker-bearing over-limit JD; round 2 on the same track
found that empty-string evidence was a universal substring match and
pinned empty-means-missing; round 1 on the web track moved the receipt
markup assertions onto comment-blanked source so a gutted receipt could
not hide behind a comment; and round 1 on the compiler track added the
end-to-end proof that a stored pre-F-62 package still reads through the
real database re-validation path with every dimension's evidence absent.
